Frequently asked questions

Is Tufan a video platform or a blog?

Tufan is a channel-based publishing front-end. Each post can be a video, article, or short. We use Ghost memberships and visibility to gate content.

What are the access levels?

Creators can mark posts as Public, Members only, Paid-members only, or restrict to specific tier(s).

How do I start a channel?

Create a free account, then head to /apply or contact us. We’ll get you set up with a handle and Creator Studio access.

Do I need to use Ghost?

Yes, Tufan connects to Ghost Content/Admin APIs behind the scenes. You’ll manage pricing, tiers and site-wide settings in Ghost Admin.

Can I export my content?

Absolutely. Your content lives in Ghost and can be exported any time.
